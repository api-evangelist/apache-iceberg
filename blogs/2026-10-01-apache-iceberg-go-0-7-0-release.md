The Apache Iceberg community is pleased to announce version 0.7.0 of iceberg-go . This release covers approximately four months of development since the 0.6.0 release in May 2026 and is the result of merging nearly 750 PRs from 53 contributors , including 33 first-time contributors . See the full changelog for the complete list of changes.
